Step-by-Step Journey

How a Custom Commission Unfolds

Estimated production timeline: 4 to 8 weeks depending on dimensions, relief complexity, and frame carving.

Step 01 · Week 1

Artwork Consultation

Share your preferred deity, wall dimensions, room lighting, and framing style. Our studio curator recommends proportions and finish options.

Step 02 · Week 2

Line Drawing & Approval

Our master artist prepares a traditional charcoal and pencil composition on cloth-mounted jackfruit wood for your review before relief work begins.

Step 03 · Weeks 3–6

Sukkan Relief & 24K Gold Leaf

Raised limestone embossing is sculpted, uncut Jaipur stones are set, and pure 24K Thanjavur gold foil is gilded alongside natural mineral pigments.

Step 04 · Weeks 7–8

Teak Framing & Insured Delivery

Final portrait detailing is completed, housed in your chosen carved teakwood frame, certified, and crated for insured domestic or global delivery.

24K Gold & Gemstones

Traditional Gold-Leaf Detailing & Finish Options

Choose between radiant Classic 24K Bright Gold for luminous contemporary interiors or Antique Temple Patina for an aged heirloom aesthetic. Every piece includes genuine uncut Jaipur stones and a signed certificate of authenticity.
