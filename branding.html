Materials & Mastery

24K Gold Leaf, Gemstones & Framing

A closer look at the sacred materials—limestone sukkan, pure 24-karat gold foil, Jaipur kundan stones, and carved Burma teakwood—that define every Tanjore masterpiece.

Sukkan & Gilding

Raised Gesso Relief & 24K Gold Foil

Traditional Thanjavur embossing uses a hand-ground paste of raw limestone chalk (sukkan) and natural gum arabic applied with fine cones to sculpt arches, crowns, and temple pillars in three-dimensional relief.

Once cured, pure 24-karat Thanjavur gold leaf is delicately cut and burnished onto the raised motifs, creating an unmistakable luminous depth that never tarnishes.
