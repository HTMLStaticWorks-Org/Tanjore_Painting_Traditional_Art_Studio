Traditional Technique

Six Sacred Stages of Creation

Every Tanjore masterpiece takes 4 to 8 weeks of patient, unhurried studio craft.

Stage 01

Jackfruit Wood & Cloth

Unbleached cotton fabric is mounted onto seasoned jackfruit wood using natural tamarind-seed paste and chalk primer.

Stage 02

Classical Line Drawing

Deity proportions, temple arches, and ornamental borders are hand-drawn following traditional Shilpa Shastra canons.

Stage 03

Sukkan Gesso Relief

Raised three-dimensional arches, crowns, and jewellery motifs are sculpted by hand with fine limestone chalk paste.

Stage 04

Jaipur Stone Setting

Uncut ruby, emerald, and white kundan gemstones are carefully inlaid into the sculpted relief before gilding begins.

Stage 05

24K Gold-Leaf Gilding

Pure 24-karat Thanjavur gold foil is hand-cut and burnished over the raised sukkan work for an enduring sacred glow.

Stage 06

Mineral Pigments & Framing

Fine facial expressions and rich mineral colours are painted last, then framed in carved Burma teakwood with museum glass.
