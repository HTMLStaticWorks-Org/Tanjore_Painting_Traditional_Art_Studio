Common Questions

Commission & Studio FAQs

Everything collectors and patrons ask about 24K gold-leaf authenticity, timelines, framing, and worldwide delivery.

Do you use genuine 24K gold leaf in your Tanjore paintings?

Yes. Every painting is hand-gilded using authentic 24-karat Thanjavur gold leaf over raised limestone sukkan relief, accompanied by a signed certificate of authenticity from our studio.

How long does a custom Tanjore painting commission take?

Depending on dimensions and relief complexity, most commissions are completed within 4 to 8 weeks. We share progress photographs at the line-drawing and 24K gold-leaf stages for your approval.

Can I customize the deity iconography, size, and teakwood frame?

Yes. You may specify your preferred deity composition, exact wall dimensions, bright or antique gold patina, backdrop color, and a Classic Mani or Carved Chettinad Burma teakwood frame.

Do you ship framed Tanjore paintings internationally?

Yes. All artworks are packed in custom-fitted, foam-cushioned wooden art crates with museum-grade glazing and insured door-to-door delivery across India and worldwide.

How should I care for a 24K gold-leaf Tanjore painting?

Display your painting away from direct moisture and harsh noon sunlight. Gently dust the teakwood frame and protective glass with a dry, soft microfiber cloth—no chemical cleaners are needed.
