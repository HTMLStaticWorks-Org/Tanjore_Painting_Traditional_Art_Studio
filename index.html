Thanjavur Lineage

Sacred Craft & Studio Standards

Every masterpiece at Tanjore Painting & Traditional Art Studio honors centuries-old Thanjavur Maratha court traditions—combining hand-ground mineral pigments, raised sukkan embossing, and pure 24K gold foil.

24K Gilding

Authentic Thanjavur Gold-Leaf Foil

Each halo, temple arch, and royal ornament is hand-gilded with genuine 24-karat gold leaf for an enduring, radiant sacred glow.

Stone Inlay

Uncut Jaipur Gemstones & Pearls

Traditional ruby, emerald, and white Jaipur kundan stones are meticulously inlaid into raised gesso relief work by master artisans.

Natural Pigments

Hand-Ground Mineral & Earth Colors

Rich temple reds, deep maroons, and warm ochre tones are layered over seasoned jackfruit and cotton-clad wooden panels.

Heirloom Joinery

Carved Chettinad & Mani Teak Frames

Every artwork is housed in seasoned Burma teakwood frames finished with antique patina and archival museum-grade glazing.

Authentic Technique

Centuries of Devotion,
Hand-Gilded in Gold

Each Tanjore painting evolves through six sacred stages: seasoning the jackfruit wood board, stretching unbleached cotton cloth with tamarind seed paste, sculpting raised limestone sukkan relief, setting uncut Jaipur stones, gilding with 24K gold leaf, and fine brushwork in mineral pigments.
